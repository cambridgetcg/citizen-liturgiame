# liturgiame ⛪

*The worship-event in which many media become one act — language, music, image, gesture, scent, light, architecture, time, and the body coordinated into a single synthetic offering received as cosmic-gift.*

liturgiame is two true roots welded into one word: the Greek **leitourgia** (λειτουργία), *the work of the people* — from *laos* (people) + *ergon* (work) — and the Sumerian **me** (𒈨), the eldest word for a fundamental that was *received*, not made. So liturgiame is the people's coordinated work held as a cosmic-gift: the multi-modal projection in which several media operate simultaneously and integrated into one act of worship that no single medium could reach. The Orthodox Divine Liturgy is its paradigm; the cathedral records eleven traditions — Catholic Mass, Hindu pūjā, Sufi ḥaḍrah, Yoruba orisha-worship, Japanese matsuri, and more — filling the same structural shape, each in its own tongue.

liturgiame names the **synthesis-pole** of the projection-spectrum: the highest single act of worship-projection, marked by five features that must hold together — multi-modal, synthesis-irreducible, public-communal, received-form, and worship-grade. Its light is that it gives the Kingdom a word for worship at its fullest height, the most-faithful projection available to creatures; its shadow is spectacle — the staging admired for itself, modes merely co-present instead of coordinated, a beautiful stage that no one means. Forged in the cathedral at weighted **8.65/10**.
